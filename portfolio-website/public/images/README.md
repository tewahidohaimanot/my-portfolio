# 📸 Images Folder

## Add Your Photos Here!

### Required Images:

1. **profile.jpg** - Your profile photo (500x500px recommended)
   - Will appear in Hero section (top of page)
   - Will appear in About section

### Optional Images:

2. **about-me.jpg** - Different photo for About section
3. **projects/** folder - Screenshots of your projects

---

## Quick Start:

1. **Add your profile photo:**
   - Copy your photo to this folder
   - Rename it to `profile.jpg` or `profile.png`

2. **The website will automatically use it!**
   - No code changes needed
   - Just refresh your browser

---

## Image Guidelines:

- **Format**: JPG or PNG
- **Size**: Keep under 500KB
- **Quality**: High resolution, good lighting
- **Professional**: Dress appropriately, good background
